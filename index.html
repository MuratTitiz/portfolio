Learning experience designer and UX researcher in Tokyo

Children and adults want the same thing: an experience with tegotae and meaning.

I watch how people actually learn and use things, design from educational and psychological principles, then test with real users and fix what doesn't work.

Tegotae (手応え): the real, in-your-hands feeling that your effort produced a result.

I have 6+ years in Japanese education, including running a language school's operations, and before that a psychology degree and four years of behavioral research. At school I interview parents, test tools with teachers and learners, and build the systems that fix what I find. On my own, I'm building Prosper Academy, a financial literacy curriculum tested with 20+ learners. I work in English and Japanese (JLPT N1).
