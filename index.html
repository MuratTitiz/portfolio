School Audit: Parent Retention & Revenue Clarity

You know some parents are unhappy. You see enrollment flat, trial requests shrinking, or students quitting after two or three months. Most schools don't know the real cause because they're not asking the right people, in the right way. That's what this audit is for.

What the audit involves

We conduct structured interviews with three groups:

  • Parents (current and former) — why they joined, what surprised them, why some left
  • Staff and teachers — recurring complaints, where communication breaks down, what they find hardest to manage
  • You (the owner) — your revenue targets, your read on where the gaps are, what you've already tried

We also review your parent journey end-to-end — from trial inquiry through the first months of enrollment — and identify where families are most likely to disengage.

What you get

  • Specific problems, not observations — diagnoses with enough detail to act on immediately
  • Revenue impact assessment — which issues are costing you students and which are just friction
  • A prioritised first fix — the highest-impact, lowest-effort intervention, with a rationale for why it comes first
  • Systems assessment — whether your current tools and workflows are part of the problem
