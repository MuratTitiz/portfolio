Learning experience designer and UX researcher based in Tokyo

Good learning should leave you with something to show for it.

I start by seeing how people learn and what gets in their way. Then I use ideas from education and psychology to shape an experience, try it with real people, and improve what isn't working.

Tegotae (手応え): the feeling that your actions made a real difference.

I've spent more than six years working in education in Japan, including managing the day-to-day operations of a language school. Before that, I studied psychology and spent four years in behavioral research. In my current work, I speak with parents, test tools with teachers and students, and build practical ways to address the problems we uncover. I'm also developing Prosper Academy, a financial literacy curriculum that I've tested with more than 20 learners. I work in both English and Japanese (JLPT N1).
